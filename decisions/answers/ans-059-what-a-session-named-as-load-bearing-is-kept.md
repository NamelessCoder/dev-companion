# D-ANS-059 — What a session named as load-bearing is kept

**The invocation notes beside the suites and the verdict checklist a triage
makes a session read stay, because a session named both as what changed what it
did.**

Seven feedback came out of one triage-and-patch session, and they draw a
boundary: the strengths name six tools and both skills, the failures name two,
and both of those reach a host this repository does not own. Every cost the
session named was on the side this server reads rather than computes.

## Evidence

- `feedback/2026-08-05-033954` is the strengths half, and it is specific.
  `typo3_project_describe` gave the PHP floor before a file was opened, which is
  why the verification went through `runTests.sh` at all; `typo3_test_run_guide`
  gave the `CI=true` prefix, the `-b docker` switch and "a green that ran over
  no files is not a green", which the session acted on; `typo3_hint_lookup` said
  the expectations hide in fixtures; `typo3_rule_lookup` saved an unnecessary
  `.rst` file; `typo3_changelog_lookup` established the area had not been
  reworked since 2019.
- The same debrief credits both skills with changing what was done rather than
  describing it: two verdicts instead of one wrong one, and a test proved red
  before the fix.
- The four failures are `typo3_forge_lookup` three times and
  `typo3_gerrit_lookup` once —
  [`D-ANS-055`](ans-055-a-change-answers-for-an-issue-its-commit-message-names.md),
  [`D-ANS-056`](ans-056-a-search-hit-is-filled-from-the-issue-it-is.md),
  [`D-ANS-057`](ans-057-what-hangs-off-an-issue-is-named.md)
  and the backlog interface that had landed an hour before the session ran.
- The fifth cost, `feedback/2026-08-05-033924`, is a fact that lives on neither
  side: which release lines are maintained is not in the checkout and not in the
  corpus, and the session inferred it from 40 commits.
- Nothing computed here was reported as wrong. The one error the session names
  against itself — running host `phpunit` after being told the floor was 8.5 —
  is a fact this server had already given it.

## Decided

- The strengths are read as a boundary and not as a decision holding. What is
  confirmed is where the two kinds of answer sit; a decision is confirmed by a
  recorded run, and none of these calls is one
  ([`D-FBK-018`](../feedback/fbk-018-a-strength-is-evidence-about-a-boundary-not-about-a-decision.md)).
- What the session named as load-bearing is what may not be quietly dropped: the
  invocation notes beside the suites in `typo3_test_run_guide`, and the verdict
  checklist that `typo3-core-issue-triage` makes a session read before it
  writes.
- A reader over a foreign API owes more than a reader over the corpus. What it
  hands back is held against what the source actually said, because nothing
  downstream can tell a confident wrong answer from a right one, and both skills
  are written to stop work on a hit.
- The three fixes are that rule applied once. What is not decided is whether it
  generalises to the other network readers — the manuals and the contribution
  services — and no evidence here says either way.

## Assumed

- One session is one reading. The split is clean in these seven and that is what
  makes it worth writing down, not proof that the next debrief will land the
  same way.
- The costs are properties of the sources rather than of their age. Both failing
  tools are among the newest here, and a simpler reading is that they have had
  the least use.

## Wrong if

- A later debrief reports a computed answer — a hint, a catalog entry, a guide —
  as the one that misled it, which would say the boundary is elsewhere.
- The network readers stop being reported at all after these fixes, which would
  say the split was about three defects rather than about a kind of answer.
- Somebody drops one of the named strengths and the next session reports the
  cost the note was carrying.

## Since then

The next debrief landed the same way, which the first **Assumed** said this
could not promise: a later triage session in the same checkout names only
computed answers as load-bearing — a refusal carrying its cause and the command
that fixes it, the flag that explained a failure printed by the host
interpreter, the same suite line the first debrief named, and a changelog entry
reached from a bare identifier.

One of them cuts across the split rather than along it.
