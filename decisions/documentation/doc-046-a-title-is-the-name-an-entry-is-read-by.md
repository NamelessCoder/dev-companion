# D-DOC-046 — A title is the name an entry is read by

**A title says one thing in twelve words or so, and what it is not is the
statement said again.**

The statement is held to thirty words and fails a check. The title had no
measure at all, so it grew into the sentence below it.

## Evidence

- Measured on 2026-08-23 over 446 decisions. The mean title is 65 characters and
  twelve words, the longest 116 characters, and 227 run past twelve words or
  join two claims.
- A title reuses 46% of its own words from its own statement, and 68 of the 446
  reuse three quarters or more. That is the mechanism: with nothing to be
  instead, the title became the statement.
- 89 join two claims outright, with a comma-and, a dash or a semicolon — "The
  Extbase fork is delivered by the content-element intent, and it forks on the
  request rather than on the category". Every one of the first twelve read had
  its second clause in the statement already, near-verbatim.
- The requirement corpus is the control, and it is healthy: 222 titles, mean
  nine words, fourteen past twelve, two joining two claims. A requirement's
  title names a demand and its statement makes it, which is the split this
  restores for a decision.

## Decided

- Twelve words as the measure, and `bin/cli prose:check` reports it beside the
  sentence count. Nothing fails: 227 entries broke it the day it was written,
  and a rule that many entries break is a rewrite wearing a rule's clothes —
  which is `D-DOC-002`'s reading of the same problem one corpus over.
- 69 titles were shortened here: the ones that both ran long and joined two
  claims, where the reading is unambiguous because the dropped clause is in the
  statement. The 23 that join two claims and stay short are left alone — "Every
  stage is a directory, and closing is none" is one thought with its
  consequence, and cutting it would lose the point rather than a repetition.
- The 153 that run long without joining are the report's to carry. Shortening
  one is a judgement about which words earn their place, and a sweep would take
  the distinguishing half off the family of entries titled "… is a gap this
  server owns".
- File names are left alone. A slug is a name rather than a claim about today,
  and renaming 69 files would rewrite every link that names one for nothing a
  reader gains.

## Assumed

- That the second clause of a joined title is always in the statement. It was in
  the twelve read side by side, and the rest were shortened on the same reading
  without checking each statement word for word.
- That twelve is the number. It is where the requirement corpus already sits
  rather than a measure taken of what reads well, and what would settle it is a
  listing nobody can scan at that length.

## Wrong if

- The count sits where it is for a month while entries are written and read.
  Then reporting is not enough for a title either, and the measure needs a place
  in the work rather than a line in a check — the same failure `D-DOC-002` names
  for the sentence count.
- A shortened title turns out to have carried the half a listing was read for,
  and somebody has to open the entry to tell two apart. `D-ANS-045` was
  shortened into `R-ANS-020`'s title on the first pass and given another, so the
  failure has already happened once.
- Titles start being written to the measure and stop saying what was settled.
  Twelve words is where a name fits, not a budget to spend.

## Since then

123 more titles were shortened once the file name became the title's, and what
the measure counts fell from 227 to 55 — the 23 that join two claims and stay
short, and about 19 a word or two long. That is where it stopped, and the shape
is the reason: the affirmative "A is B, not C" is what the format asks for, and
cutting the second half takes the half that tells an entry from its neighbours.
The file names moved with them, which is the pressure `D-DOC-047` assumed.

The family left to the report was retitled on the other half of the rule
(`D-DOC-056`): 38 titles got longer, and the trade this entry did not price is
that the distinguishing half was kept verbatim, so what the extra words buy is a
listing that reads as one kind of entry.
