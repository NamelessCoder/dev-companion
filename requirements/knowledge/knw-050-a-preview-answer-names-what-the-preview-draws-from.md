# R-KNW-050 — A preview answer names what the preview draws from

**A backend preview answer names the field kinds the preview draws its content
from, and says the preview is what two instances of one element are told apart
by.**

Where the template is registered, what it is handed and what the header already
draws are three answers about everything except the output, and a preview
reading "Testimonials element" satisfies all three. The demand they leave open
is the one an editor pays for.

The field kinds are named one at a time, because that is what decides a line of
the template: the text columns, file relations, link fields, a select's chosen
item and the children of an inline relation. "Show a summary" is advice and
changes nothing about what somebody writes.

It carries no version of its own. The evidence is `theme_camino`, which ships on
14 alone, but what a preview owes the editor is not a property of a major — how
a field is reached is the half that is bound, and `R-KNW-041` carries it.

## From

A session that built a TYPO3 14 testimonials element and shipped a preview
naming the element, having followed every preview statement the corpus had; the
rule it was missing came from the user instead (2026-08-01).
