# Route the Composer root step at the hint that owns the installer keys

**Serves:** feedback/2026-08-17-212152-the-decisions-that-cannot-be-revised-are-all.md
**Priority:** normal

Step 3 on `D-SKL-047`: step 1 of `typo3-development-installation` sends the
caller to the manual and to the installer package the manifest has not installed
yet, while `extension-repository-installation` already states the keys and no
skill names it. Name that id at the step with what it alone answers, in the form
steps 2 and 5 use, and take out the paraphrase of it that step 1 keeps — the
layout key that warns rather than errors, the package the core requires itself,
the empty extension directory. Whether the manual half of the sentence stays is
the reading: `typo3_documentation_lookup` at the version being installed is what
the report says returned page titles and changed nothing, and the hint is the
owner either way. `SkillTest::anInstallationIsBuiltInDependencyOrderAndHandsOverOnceItAnswers`
already holds the other two steps to that form and is where this one lands.
