# Reach the recent end of the backlog, where a duplicate is

**Serves:** feedback/2026-08-24-225214-no-way-to-establish-that-an-issue-has-not-been.md
**Priority:** normal

Judged on 2026-08-27 and trimmed. Step 1b, the shape is missing, and `D-ANS-116`
is the decision with the measurements: `open` orders only by `oldest` and by
`stale`, so both pages are the neglected end, while a duplicate of a defect
somebody has just found is at the other one.

Build the way to that end on the enumeration `typo3_forge_lookup` already has —
a further ordering and a recency window, not a fourth way into the tool.
`/issues.json` answers both today, measured through `Http\Fetch`:
`sort=created_on:desc` reaches 2482 open issues newest first and
`created_on=>=2026-08-01` reaches 96.

Two things the reading has to settle, because neither is decided yet. An area
does not fit one page — Backend User Interface holds 437 open issues against a
`limit` of 50 with no offset — so say what the caller does with the rest. And an
issue filed under no Category is in no area at all, which is what #110533 is, so
a route narrowed only by `category` cannot reach the case this feedback is
about.

Trim `ForgeLookup::searched()` with it. "Reading those subjects is what settles
whether somebody already reported this" was written from an area holding 26 and
overstates what the call delivers above the page bound.
