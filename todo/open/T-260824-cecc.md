# The guides list only exists inside one tool's answer, and the page that fitted my task went unread

Give every guide reference the line that says when to read it.
`Schema::guideReference` carries `id` and `title` alone, and `Schema::nextTool`
beside it already has that shape in its `when`, which is the form to copy.
Answered on 2026-08-27 against handing a page over whole and against waiting for
more reports: six sessions have now read a page's name and opened nothing — from
the brief and a hint, from the orientation answer three times, and from a skill
body. What the line buys is a `documentId` that is a decision instead of a
title, and what it does not buy is the return to a page already read, which the
entry of 2026-08-27 in
[`D-GUI-012`](../../decisions/guides/gui-012-the-brief-names-the-guide-the-recognized-work-belongs-to.md)
measured on `feedback/2026-08-24-225153`. That entry's third **Wrong if** is
what it is built against, and the commit that ships it archives the two feedback
it answers, `2026-08-24-140239` and `2026-08-24-173211`, whose cards are this
one and [`T-260824-87b2`](T-260824-87b2.md). What it leaves open is the half
`feedback/2026-08-27-145428` names, judged on its own card: the field is
`guides` and the tool that reads it is `typo3_rule_lookup` taking a
`documentId`, which no name joins.
