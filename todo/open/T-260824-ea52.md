# The label miss answers for the resource rather than for the words

**Serves:** feedback/2026-08-24-225129-a-wrong-resource-path-and-a-resource-with-no.md
**Priority:** normal

Step 4 of the ladder, wording, judged into `D-ANS-016` on 2026-08-27: `resource`
narrows the labels the way `version` narrows the changelog, and
`LabelLookup::answer()` counts the terms after filtering by it, so a path that
exists nowhere reports every word of the query at 0.

Give the label miss the two things `typo3_changelog_lookup` already answers a
narrowed miss with. `termCountsWithoutTheNarrowing` is the counts taken outside
the resource, returned only where a word reaches there and nothing inside it.
The other is the sibling's `tags`, one axis over: where the named resource
carries no labels at all, name the resources that do, so a guessed path can be
replaced by one that exists.
