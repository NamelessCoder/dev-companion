# A todo's head is parsed out of bold prose lines

**Serves:** todo/
**Priority:** normal

A requirement and a decision open with YAML front matter that
`TYPO3\DevCompanion\Upkeep\Entry` reads in one place, and a todo opens with
labelled bold lines that `Todo::head()` pulls apart with its own regexes over
`**Serves:**`, `**Priority:**`, `**Every:**`, `**Checked:**`, `**Run:**` and
`**Waiting on:**` — the head of a todo is data read out of prose, which is what
this repository says it does not do. Read whether the same front matter fits
here and, where it does, move every file below `todo/` onto it: the six fields
become keys `Entry::head()` returns, the title stays the `#` heading, the
regexes and the indented-continuation handling in `src/Upkeep/Todo.php` come out
in favour of the reader that already exists, and `todo:check`, `todo:list`,
`todo:next` and `todo/readme.md` are held to the new shape in the same commit.
Two things the step turns on and neither is recalled: whether the front matter
carries an `id` and a `title` the way an entry's does when a todo's id is
already its file name, and what writes `checked` back after a recurring todo has
run, which is a line a session edits today.
