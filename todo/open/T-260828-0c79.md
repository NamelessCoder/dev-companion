# Compact the dated sections of decisions/code/

`bin/cli decisions:check` names the sections over the measure; in
`decisions/code/` there are 3 of them across 3 entries, 56 lines in all. Compact
each onto `D-DOC-066`: a reading that changed nothing becomes its date in
`readings:` and its section goes, and a reading that changed something stays as
at most twelve lines saying what changed. What will not fit is a finding rather
than prose to trim — it belongs in **Decided**, in **Wrong if**, or in an entry
of its own. Read the section before deleting it: the ones worth keeping are the
ones a later entry already names.
