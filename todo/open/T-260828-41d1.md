# Compact the dated sections of decisions/audience/

`bin/cli decisions:check` names the sections over the measure; in
`decisions/audience/` there are 18 of them across 12 entries, 556 lines in all.
Compact each onto `D-DOC-066`: a reading that changed nothing becomes its date
in `readings:` and its section goes, and a reading that changed something stays
as at most twelve lines saying what changed. What will not fit is a finding
rather than prose to trim — it belongs in **Decided**, in **Wrong if**, or in an
entry of its own. Read the section before deleting it: the ones worth keeping
are the ones a later entry already names.
