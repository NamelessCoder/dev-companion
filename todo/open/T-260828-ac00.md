# The crossing into a fix says the sweep is owed again

`skills/base.md` exempts a workflow that writes nothing from the deprecation
sweep and closes the exemption in the same paragraph, minutes before the moment
it applies: a review that took it, was then asked for the fix and re-entered
through `typo3-extension-health` worked every item without the sweep. Put the
clause where the crossing is marked — step 9 of that skill, where the items are
taken in order, or step 10, which calls the crossing the transition itself — so
a list built out of a review says the sweep is owed before the first item is
worked, on the paths the items name. `D-SKL-049` carries this as its fourth
sighting and defers only the general closing gate, so this is the point-of-use
correction the three before it were. The report also asks that a skill opening
with "work through references/base.md" distinguish reading the document from
running its steps for the files about to be written; decide that with the same
change, because it is the same crossing seen from the other side.
