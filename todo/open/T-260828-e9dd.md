# The brief says which of the skills it names are stale

`Installer::outdated()` is computed once, in `Server\Entrypoint`, and becomes
the prefix on the initialize instructions; `TaskGuide` names the skills that own
the task and says nothing about them. Give the brief a field beside `skills`
naming the ones a project's published copy is behind on, read through
`Instance::startedIn()` the way the entrypoint reads `getcwd()`, and assert it
from a project whose record carries a digest that no longer matches. Then write
the digest into each published skill, so a session can quote what it read —
`Installer::publishSkill()` is where a copy is made and `Installer::digest()` is
what `outdated()` compares. `D-SKL-086` has both shapes and why the initialize
notice stays where it is.
