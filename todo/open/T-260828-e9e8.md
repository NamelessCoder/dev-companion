# Compact the dated sections of decisions/documentation/

`bin/cli decisions:check` names the sections over the measure; in
`decisions/documentation/` there are 25 of them across 22 entries, 711 lines in
all. Compact each onto `D-DOC-066`: a reading that changed nothing becomes its
date in `readings:` and its section goes, and a reading that changed something
stays as at most twelve lines saying what changed. What will not fit is a
finding rather than prose to trim — it belongs in **Decided**, in **Wrong if**,
or in an entry of its own. Read the section before deleting it: the ones worth
keeping are the ones a later entry already names.
