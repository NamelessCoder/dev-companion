# Dissolve the two entries that became journals

`D-FBK-018` carries 20 dated sections and 1 485 lines of them, `D-FBK-021` ten,
because every reading of one kind was appended to the entry stating the rule for
that kind. Move each reading to the entry whose boundary it is about — a
strength is evidence about a boundary, which is what `D-FBK-018` itself says —
and leave in these two only what was learned about their own rule, at twelve
lines a section. Where a reading bears on no entry, it is the archived feedback
that keeps it and nothing is written. `documentation/records/judging.rst` says
there is no journal beside the archive, which is what these two had become.
