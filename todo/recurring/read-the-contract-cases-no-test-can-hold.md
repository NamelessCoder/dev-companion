# Read the contract cases no test can hold

**Serves:** scenarios/, decisions/
**Every:** 14 days
**Checked:** 2026-08-02
**Run:** bin/cli scenarios:contract SKILL-09

Five cases fall back on being read by hand, and what they are waiting for is the
same thing every time: whether a session does what the case says, which no test
here reaches. `SKILL-09` is the one to start from — it says in as many words
that it measures the rest — then `SKILL-07` and `EXT-01`, whose crossings
`D-EVI-002` holds by their wording, `EXT-03`, where nothing makes a session pass
`workflow="project"` at all, and `SITE-01`, where the boundary is stated and
nothing keeps the checklist under it from contradicting the statement. Read each
against the skills and the answers as they are today, not against the last
reading: the proxy is a sentence in a file that a reorganization satisfies while
the behaviour goes. What a reading finds is a feedback, a requirement, or a line
in the case saying it still holds and on what evidence — and the date above,
which is what keeps five sessions from re-reading the same five files.
